---
name: Neurodiversity Language Guidance
description: How to frame neurodiversity in this project — avoid clinical labels
type: feedback
---

Do not use clinical terms like "autism", "ADHD", or "special needs" in copy or design discussion. The Syntonie audience includes non-diagnosed neurodivergent people who are not seeking clinical support.

**Why:** Syntonie addresses curious builders and creative makers who happen to have non-typical cognitive styles. Using clinical labels is exclusionary and misrepresents the audience. These are explorers, not patients.

**How to apply:** Use "curious builders", "explorers with non-linear thinking", "people who prefer visible structure". Neurodiversity is a design constraint (high contrast, visible affordances, minimal ambiguity), not an audience label.
