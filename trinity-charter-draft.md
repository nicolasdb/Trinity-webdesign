# The Trinity: Syntonie · Politype · OpenFab

## A Common Vision

**We believe democracy, technology, and making belong together.**

Not as separate worlds. Not as abstract concepts. But as living practice — governance in the hands of people, technology in service of communities, making as the bridge between idea and reality.

The challenges of our time — polarization, surveillance capitalism, democratic erosion — won't be solved by one discipline alone. They require a new ecology: thinkers, builders, and experimenters working in concert.

This is the Trinity.

## Syntonie ☂️

### Genesis

Founded in 2012, Syntonie emerged from a simple observation: the relationship between humans, technology, and environment was breaking. The word itself — *syntonie* — means "being in resonance." Vibrating together.

Syntonie was created to be the bridge: not a company, not a traditional NGO, but an ASBL (non-profit) that holds space for emerging experiments in how we live together.

### Role

**The umbrella. The legal entity. The vision keeper.**

Syntonie owns assets, signs contracts, ensures compliance, and maintains strategic coherence across the ecosystem. It doesn't operate day-to-day — it enables.

- Governance & legal structure
- Strategic vision (resonance of human + tech + environment)
- Fundraising & partnerships
- EU sustainable goals alignment (digital transformation + green)
- Asset ownership (tools, space, equipment)

### Do ✅

- Think long-term
- Maintain legal compliance
- Own assets on behalf of the ecosystem
- Connect partners and funders
- Ensure coherence across entities
- Enable sub-entities to exist and thrive

### Don't ❌

- Run day-to-day operations
- Deliver workshops directly
- Make things (that's OpenFab)
- Prototype governance (that's Politype)
- Micromanage sub-entities

### Who We're Looking For

Strategic thinkers. Legal minds. Partnership builders. People who see the long game and want to hold space for experiments they don't control.

## Politype 🫰

### Genesis

Democracy isn't finished. It's in permanent beta.

Politype was born from a question: *What if we treated governance like open-source software?* Prototype. Test. Iterate. Break. Rebuild.

The name comes from *polity* (political organization) + *type* (prototype, archetype, type). Multiple governance types within one framework. Democracy as design problem.

Politype is where the book *Plurality* (E. Glen Weyl, Audrey Tang) meets practice. Intellectual foundation meets prototyping lab.

### Role

**The heart. The experimenters. The democracy prototypers.**

Politype designs and tests governance mechanisms: game-based decision-making, participatory tools, civic engagement frameworks. Not ideology — methodology.

**Positioning:** Located 900 meters from the European Parliament — prototyping democracy at the edge of where it's practiced.

- Game-based governance methodology
- Democratizing complex tech
- Neuro-inclusive participation
- Physical token design
- Simplifying complexity

### Do ✅

- Design governance games
- Prototype democratic tools
- Host civic workshops
- Simplify complex systems
- Test, iterate, break, rebuild
- Make participation playable

### Don't ❌

- Be a general makerspace
- Be a board game club
- Do corporate gamification
- Advocate for specific political parties
- Take ideological positions

### Who We're Looking For

Game designers. Civic technologists. Facilitators. Policy makers. People who believe democracy should be participatory, not just representative. Those who make complex systems accessible.

## OpenFab 🧰

### Genesis

In 2012, Syntonie asked: where do ideas become real? The answer was OpenFab — a physical space where people can access tools, learn skills, and build things together.

A fablab, but more than a fablab. A community space that attracts neurodiversity, embraces informal learning, and walks the talk of making.

### Role

**The hands. The workshop. The physical expression.**

OpenFab operates the makerspace: tools, space, workshops, community. It walks the talk. While Syntonie thinks and Politype experiments, OpenFab builds.

- Physical space management
- Tool access & safety training
- Workshops & informal learning
- Maker community cultivation
- Tangible production

### Do ✅

- Provide tools (owned by Syntonie)
- Host events and workshops
- Teach making skills
- Enable creation and prototyping
- Welcome neurodiversity
- Build community

### Don't ❌

- Set legal strategy (that's Syntonie)
- Run governance experiments (that's Politype)
- Own assets

### Who We're Looking For

Makers. Facilitators. Tool whisperers. People who love seeing ideas become physical reality. Those who believe access to tools should be universal.

## How They Work Together

```
Syntonie (Head)
    │
    ├── holds the vision, owns the assets
    │
    ├── Politype (Heart)
    │       │
    │       └── experiments with governance
    │
    └── OpenFab (Hands)
            │
            └── makes things real
```

**Separation of concerns:**

- Syntonie governs, doesn't operate
- Politype prototypes, doesn't build
- OpenFab builds, doesn't govern

**Flow of resources:**

- Syntonie → provides legal structure, funding, assets
- Politype → provides governance tools, participation methodology
- OpenFab → provides physical space, tools, making expertise

**Each entity is autonomous within its domain. Together, they form a complete organism.**
