## Audience Segmentation

### Syntonie — The Institutional Page

**Primary audience:** Erasmus National Agency, Commune d'Ixelles, institutional partners, funders
**Secondary:** Visitors arriving from OpenFab/Politype pages wanting to understand the umbrella

**What they're looking for:**
- Legal clarity — ASBL since when, governed how
- Track record — evidence of capacity (dates, numbers, project names)
- The operating model — how three entities interrelate (the graph, in institutional language)
- Who to contact for partnerships, co-financing, framework agreements

**Emotional register:** Trust. Sobriety. "These people are serious and structured."

**Narrative arc:** Legitimacy → Model → Evidence → Ecosystem → Partnership

**Content implications:**
- Role section: "Research & Methodology" framing, NOT "GameLab" (too jargon-y for institutional audience)
- Trinity section: explain the operating model, not tease sister sites — show the graph logic
- Contact section: institutional language — "partenariats institutionnels, co-financement, accords-cadres"
- Numbers as evidence of capacity, not as marketing bragging

---

### OpenFab — The Maker Page

**Primary audience:** Local makers, creative technologists, curious neighbors (rue Gray / Ixelles)
**Secondary:** Schools, FabLab network peers, potential workshop participants

**What they're looking for:**
- Inspiration — what's been built here, what *could* be built
- Access — machines, tools, space, how to join
- Community — peers, skill-sharing, events, belonging
- Services — vitrine, agenda, networks, makerspace (per the RDF diagram)

**Emotional register:** Desire. Warmth. "Come make something." Threshold energy.

**Narrative arc:** See → Imagine → Access → Belong → Make

**Content implications:**
- Lead with finished projects and community energy, not organizational history
- Show the space, the tools, the people
- Clear "how to join" / "how to book" paths
- Beneficiaries include "public rue — XL" (street-level visibility)

---

### Politype — The Civic Activist Page

**Primary audience:** Civic activists (DémocratieXXL types), governance practitioners, community organizers
**Secondary:** Researchers in democratic innovation, EU governance networks

**What they're looking for:**
- A place where digital democracy is being *built*, not just discussed
- Infrastructure to prototype governance ideas (physical + digital)
- Co-builders, not consumers — "come build this with us"
- Game-based methodology that makes governance tangible and testable

**Emotional register:** Invitation. "We're building this. It's not finished. Come."

**Narrative arc:** Provocation → Vision → Method → WIP Honesty → Invite

**Key pitch:**
> "La gouvernance est un prototype. On cherche des co-constructeurs."

**Content implications:**
- Lead with the WIP honesty — "en construction" IS the value proposition
- GameLab methodology belongs HERE (moved from Syntonie)
- Bridge narrative: 3D printing + electronics meet DAO + quadratic voting as game mechanics
- Do NOT use head/heart/hands language — that's internal architecture
- 900m from EU Parliament is a powerful geographic anchor
- Show the frontier: where physical fabrication meets digital governance

## Structural Model — The RDF Graph

All members belong to Syntonie ASBL. They self-organize into clusters/projects based on specific goals and needs. The three entities are **perspectives on one organism**, not separate organizations.

```
Syntonie (diamond, center) = the ontology layer / legal shelter / graph holder
  ├── OpenFab = execution arm (tools, space, making)
  ├── Politype = governance arm (facilitation XP, agenda, networks)
  └── Z entity? = extensibility proof (model scales)

People (Phil, Nicolas, Manon, X) = nodes with edges to MULTIPLE entities
Tools = shared resources accessible through the graph
Services = entity-specific offerings (vitrine, agenda, networks, makerspace)
```

**Key insight:** Syntonie doesn't hand off — it **holds the center while everything orbits.** The diamond doesn't point anywhere; everything points to and from it.

**Key insight for pages:** Each page is a *perspective* on the same organism:
- Syntonie sees the graph from **above** — structure, edges, relationships
- OpenFab sees it from **inside** — tools, making, heat
- Politype sees it from **the gap** — the missing connection that completes the circuit

## Cross-Page Navigation Logic

Visitors move between pages based on their entry point and evolving understanding:

- **Erasmus officer** lands on Syntonie → reads model → may click OpenFab for evidence of impact
- **Maker** lands on OpenFab → joins → discovers Politype through community governance
- **Civic activist** lands on Politype → realizes they need hands (OpenFab) → understands the umbrella (Syntonie)

The Trinity section on each page should reflect this — not identical 3-card layouts, but **audience-appropriate explanations of the other two entities.**

## Reframe Session (2026-03-11, party mode debrief #2)

### KILLED: Head-Heart-Hands Public Framing
- "Tête / Cœur / Mains" is internal architecture, NOT public-facing language
- "Trinity" as a word is loaded — don't foreground it in copy
- Stop name-dropping the metaphor in hero titles and section headers

### NEW: Entity Positioning

**Core insight: None of the three entities DO the thing. They all ENABLE.**

| Entity | Verb | Role |
|--------|------|------|
| Syntonie | **permet** | Legal shelter, asset ownership, strategic continuity |
| OpenFab | **équipe** | The right tool for the right job. Makerspace as kitchen for makers. |
| Politype | **prototype** | Digital democracy + game-based governance. WIP — looking for partners. |

### OpenFab Reframe
- NOT "là où les idées deviennent réelles" (generic)
- IS: the metaphorical toolbox. Physical space with mutualized equipment.
- "Un maker a besoin d'un makerspace comme un cuisinier a besoin d'une cuisine."
- "OpenFab ne fait pas non plus — elle équipe."
- More than coworking (coffee + wifi + paper printer). Makers need machines.

### Politype Reframe
- NOT "vous avez le cœur, il vous manque les mains" (patronizing)
- IS: digital democracy prototyping + game-based governance exploration
- Work-in-progress since ~2022. NOT since 2012. Looking for partners.
- Bridge: 3D printing + electronics meet DAO + quadratic voting, distilled as game mechanics
- "En construction" is not a disclaimer — it's the value proposition
- Pain point: everything accelerates but democracy requires time — the exploration paradox
- Ref: Polis 2.0 opinion mapping, attractor landscapes as emergence visualization
- Ref: plurality.net — technology serving democracy, not replacing it

### Polity Etymology (for definition card)
From Wikipedia: *Polity* = an identifiable political entity, any group of people who have a collective identity, who are organized by some form of institutionalized social relations, and have a capacity to mobilize resources. Perfect match — multiple types of governance in one framework.
